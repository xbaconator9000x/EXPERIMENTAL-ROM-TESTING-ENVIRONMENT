# Unattended native beta

The player records compatibility status, candidate field reason, native movement
witnesses, battle callback admissions, and candidate inventory/menu support.
A fixed hierarchical task tracks verified initial-party acquisition, party
health, story exploration and observed map changes. Model facts remain
hypotheses. Bounded field calibration obtains movement witnesses without
a model call on every probe. Repeated two-screen cycles get up to eight
different directional attempts across the cycle; no blind menu confirmations.
A legacy repeated-cycle terminal hold is eligible for one upgrade retry.
Actual ending and emulator error holds still require valid guidance.

On a transport/emulator failure, an independent runner replays the exact
last-good checkpoint and action once. Replayed failures are quarantined per
screen/action, with bounded local incident checkpoints, then an alternate
ordinary field approach may be attempted once. Replays are evidence of
repeatable emulator failures, not automatically evidence of a ROM bug.
No private checkpoints or ROMs are transmitted. Network failures cause
checkpoint-preserving retry with bounded backoff; private identity checks
remain mandatory. Reports include diagnostics, the objective and last
incident; initial report is sent on startup, retaining the prior decision
number. Real English v1.01 runtime proof is required. This is not a
guarantee of unattended full-game completion or arbitrary puzzle solving.

Direct dialogue-buffer extraction and long-term campaign training remain
future work; current model sees dialogue screenshots and quest notebook.
